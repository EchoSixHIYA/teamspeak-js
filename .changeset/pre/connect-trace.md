Add `trace` to `ClientOptions`: hooks called synchronously at each milestone of a client's life, for timing, metrics, logs and progress displays. For every connection attempt, the reconnect loop's included, they report `resolveStart`, `resolveDone` (with every candidate the resolver gave), `connectStart`, `connectDone` (with the local address), `handshakeDone` (with the RSA puzzle's level and duration, the packets sent again, and the server's public key for pinning), `sessionStart` (with the accepted nickname, talk power and the `initserver` parameters without any secret) and `welcomeDone` (with the channel the client landed in and the channels and clients delivered), or `attemptFailed` with the phase (`ConnectPhase`: `open`, `handshake` or `join`); then `disconnectStart`, `disconnectDone` and `sessionEnd` when a session ends, and `reconnectScheduled` and `reconnectStopped` for the reconnect loop. Every attempt ends with exactly one of `sessionStart` and `attemptFailed`, and every session with exactly one `sessionEnd`. `uploadFileData`, `downloadFileData` and `dialFileTransfer` take a `trace` of their own in `FileTransferOptions` (`dialStart`, `dialDone`, `progress`, `done`, `failed`; the transfer key is never reported), and so do the client's methods that forward to them. A hook that throws is reported like a throwing listener and never affects the connection or the transfer. `ServerCapabilities` gains `serverID`, `serverUID`, `hostMessage` and `hostMessageMode`.
